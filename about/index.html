My path runs from robotic learning to physical intelligence. I hold a PhD in Robotics and AI and have spent more than ten years in manufacturing, the built environment and the RoboTech industry across Europe and Asia.

It started with the foundations of how robots learn to move and to touch: kinematic planning and dynamic force regulation with force/torque and tactile sensors. Today it is advanced humanoids doing human-inspired manipulation and locomotion in changing environments. I have worked with robots of every kind along the way.

Many of my clients run high-value, data-sensitive projects. I make sure their data and privacy stay intact, on their own premises, while we train, test and validate state-of-the-art models and agents.

My work also covers safety-rated systems with real-time response: PLCs, IoT sensors and real-time monitoring for industrial cells.

As CTO at HumanoAI I lead the technical development of our data-collection hardware and software for training task models, turning general-purpose humanoids into expert companions on site without long hours of teaching by hand. A continual learning and adaptation layer lets the humanoid deepen its understanding of the task while it works alongside skilled workers. It is phased in gradually, so people are not replaced overnight: the robot takes on more only once it has built full knowledge of the task, the site and its surroundings. That is what physical intelligence means to me: a robot that understands the work, the place and the people around it.

I also sit on the advisory boards of technology firms and startups, helping them ground their plans in what robotics can and cannot do today.
