Read the full story

I develop on-demand edge devices for customers, building around existing boards with strain-aware mechanical design in SolidWorks and electronics in KiCad.

One device captures egocentric datasets from the human point of view, similar to Meta's Aria Gen 2 glasses but much cheaper and more accessible. Another is a novel camera system with pan-tilt-zoom capture and depth, for exocentric-view data collection used in multi-agent interaction and affordance model learning.

The devices are built on custom boards and on NVIDIA Jetson and Qualcomm Snapdragon platforms, for PREEMPT_RT-level performance and high-quality data collection for Physical AI architecture development and model training.

Alongside the hardware I developed Android and iOS apps for quick spin-up, data-collection logging, annotation and secure in-cloud training, with deployment to the robot of choice in a digital twin or on real hardware.
