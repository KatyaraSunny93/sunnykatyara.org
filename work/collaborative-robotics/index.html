What I did

  • Two Franka Emika Panda arms coordinated over libfranka TCP/IP
  • Teaching through Rhino/Grasshopper or ROS 2 with MoveIt
  • FANUC CRX-10 with adhesive gripper for medical implant parts
  • In-house middleware linking a live conveyor to the robot
Read the full story

I linked two Franka Emika Panda robots so they talk to each other over libfranka TCP/IP. Tool paths and motions can be taught in two ways: with the Robots plugin in Rhino/Grasshopper, or with a ROS 2 package using MoveIt planning for anthropomorphic manipulation.

In a separate deployment, a FANUC CRX-10 with an adhesive gripper packages annealed metal pieces for medically fabricated implants. In-house middleware connects the running industrial conveyor belt to the robot arm for visual-servoing-style scanning and packaging.
