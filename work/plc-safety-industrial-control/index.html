Read the full story

I have programmed PLCs for a wide range of industrial and consumer projects: the Siemens S7-1500 in TIA Portal, Omron TX120 in Sysmac, ABB Pluto B100 in Pluto Manager and the HCFA M1505S.

With Siemens PLCs I integrated HMIs for industrial robot arms, giving operators safety through door-locking mechanisms and spindle speed control.

With Omron PLCs and servo drive controllers I developed novel logic for sports applications that help athletes build muscle strength and extension memory through training. I built the same kind of applications on HCFA PLCs for other sports clients and institutions around the world.

With the ABB Pluto PLC I programmed system safety through its safe digital I/O, monitoring e-stops, laser scanners and light curtains for industrial additive cells at one of the biggest plastic mould manufacturers in the Netherlands.

The work runs from configuring I/O cards, drive maps and STO functions, through ladder and Structured Text logic and bespoke libraries, to Flask-based front ends that let users operate the system without getting buried in the complexity of today's PLC ecosystems.
