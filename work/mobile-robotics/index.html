What I did

  • Robotnik RB-VOGUI with vertical lift and UR10e
  • ROS 2 Jazzy: SLAM, navigation, MoveIt 2, laser-based obstacle avoidance
  • On-demand operation through a Zigbee IoT chain
  • Warthog with dual UR20 arms and Robotiq grippers for timber wall assembly and anchor drilling
  • Total-station outdoor navigation without beacons
Read the full story

The first platform is a Robotnik RB-VOGUI carrying a vertical lift and a UR10e arm for mobile manipulation. I used it for intralogistics and machine tending, loading and unloading parts between stations with extended reach and mobility.

It runs a native ROS 2 Jazzy architecture for SLAM, navigation, MoveIt 2 motion planning and dynamic laser-scanner obstacle avoidance. Operation is on demand, triggered through an installed Zigbee IoT chain.

The second platform is a Warthog mobile robot carrying two UR20 arms with Robotiq grippers. It performs dual-arm mobile assembly of timber wall installations and drills walls for anchoring. A total station provides outdoor navigation and beacon-free planning.
