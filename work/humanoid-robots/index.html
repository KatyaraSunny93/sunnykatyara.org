Read the full story

I have trained Unitree R1 and G1 robots on construction and mining tasks using data collected in the wild. The data is curated and retargeted with a proprietary mapping framework that is agnostic to robot morphology and kinematics. Policies are trained with Isaac Lab 3.0 and MuJoCo 4.0.

Beyond Unitree, I worked with EngineAI T800 robots palletizing filled crates at a manufacturing facility in Ireland, and used the Agibot G2 with its SDK to sort and package tile packets for a customer in Munich, Germany.

I also have hands-on experience with the Galbot S1, Agibot X2 and Unitree H1.
