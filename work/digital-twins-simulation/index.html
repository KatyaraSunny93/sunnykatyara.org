I build digital twins in several environments: Isaac Sim, Node.js and three.js with a custom physics backend, Blender, CoppeliaSim and Unity. They are used for synthetic data generation, policy training and sim2real transfer.

Domain randomization and domain adaptation close the fidelity gap and enable physics-aware transfer of policies from simulation to the real world, for quick rollouts, short turnaround and deployment in the wild.
